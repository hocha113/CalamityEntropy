Mod Content

Mod Wiki: https://callofvoid.wiki/
This mod is a progression expansion mod, featuring cross-mod crossover content when played with Calamity Mod.

Fully supports multiplayer play!

- This mod adds -

5 new bosses

Unique weapons (including new weapon series for Melee and Mage, as well as unique whips)

A large number of new accessories

A new Boss health bar style

A new armor reforging mechanic (optional / can be toggled)

New pets

Vanity sets of the developers and their friends

Void Toilet

More new content will continue to be added in the future!
